# Project Brief: KTCNMT 20/10 Interactive Event & Live Mini-Game Web App

## Overview
Interactive Micro-Web App for the Vietnamese Women's Day (19/10 – 20/10/2026) event of the Environmental Technology & Engineering Department (Phòng Kỹ thuật Công nghệ Môi trường - KTCNMT) – Airports Corporation of Vietnam (ACV).

## Core Objectives
1. Celebrate the 05 female members of KTCNMT and 01 female VIP guest (+1 spouse) alongside 08 male members (14 attendees total).
2. Provide personalized digital greeting cards, event timeline (including the 16:45 dress-up reminder and 18:00 dinner at The Gangs – Nguyễn Văn Trỗi), ride-sharing coordination at 17:15, and 20/10 morning milk tea pre-ordering for the 05 female members.
3. Provide +1 guest registration, transport support selection, wish submission, and internal logistics/live milk tea order summary for the 08 male members.
4. Provide a Live Wall with attendance stats, milk tea order progress, wish cards with real-time heart reactions, and ride-sharing roster.
5. Provide a synchronized real-time Mini-Game ("Túi Mù Nữ Hoàng & Lệnh Bài") across all 14 phones via 1000ms polling and Host Control Panel (`huy-duong`).
